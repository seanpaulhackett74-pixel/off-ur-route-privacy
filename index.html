OFF UR ROUTE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

OFF UR ROUTE respects your privacy. This policy explains the information the app may use, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Account information

If you create an OFF UR ROUTE account, the app may process information such as your email address, authentication identifiers, verification status and account-related settings so that you can sign in, recover your account and use member features.

Firebase and cloud services

OFF UR ROUTE uses Firebase services including Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Analytics and Crashlytics. These services may process technical identifiers, device/app information, authentication information, notification tokens, usage events and diagnostic or crash information where applicable.

Community, support and submissions

If you use Community features, Contact Admin, Add a Place or other submission features, information you choose to send may be processed so that the feature can operate, messages can be delivered, submissions can be reviewed and abuse or security issues can be handled.

Location

Location is used only when a feature needs it and you have granted permission. OFF UR ROUTE may use your device location to help with nearby searches, navigation, route-related features and location-aware suggestions. Exact live-location sharing is not enabled by default.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, OFF UR ROUTE may use a device notification token and related messaging services to deliver app notifications such as account, community, reward, service or other relevant notices.

Local app data

The app may store preferences and session-related information on your device using local or secure storage. This may include settings such as notification preferences, location hints, sign-in preferences and security-related state.

Biometric security

If you enable fingerprint or face protection, biometric verification is performed by your device operating system. OFF UR ROUTE does not receive or store your fingerprint or facial biometric template.

Analytics and crash diagnostics

OFF UR ROUTE uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage, reliability and crashes. Diagnostic information must not be used to intentionally collect passwords or private message content.

Sharing and external services

When you choose to share content or open an external service, information you choose to send may be passed to the selected app, website or navigation provider. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Offline maps and downloaded content

Where offline content is available, downloaded map or route data may be stored on your device. Removing downloaded content should not require deleting your OFF UR ROUTE account.

Data sales and advertising

OFF UR ROUTE does not sell your personal information. The current production app does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Retention and account deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for the feature, security, legal or operational purposes. You can request deletion of your account and associated account data from within the app or by using the account deletion page linked below.

Account deletion information

Children

OFF UR ROUTE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that would breach applicable law or platform requirements.

Your choices

You can control permissions such as location and notifications through the app and your device settings. You can also sign out, reset your password and delete your account using the available account controls.
