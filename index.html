OFF UR ROUTE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Location

OFF UR ROUTE may use your device location to show nearby places, route stops and things worth detouring for. Location permission can be denied or changed in your device settings.

Local app data

Saved trips, saved places, settings, Explorer Points and related activity are stored locally on your device.

Offline maps

Compatible offline map files installed by the user are stored locally on the device.

Internet services

The app uses internet-based map, routing and place services. Those services may receive normal network request information required to provide maps, routes or nearby results.

Data sales and advertising

OFF UR ROUTE does not intentionally sell personal information and does not currently contain advertising or payment features.

Permissions

The app may request internet access, network-state access, precise location and approximate location where required for map and route features.

Retention

Locally stored app data remains on the device until it is removed through the app, cleared by the user, or the app is uninstalled.

Children

OFF UR ROUTE is not specifically designed for children under 13.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be made using the developer contact details published with OFF UR ROUTE on Google Play.
