Delete Your OFF UR ROUTE Account

Last updated: 30 September 2026

You can request deletion of your OFF UR ROUTE account either directly inside the app or by contacting OFF UR ROUTE using the email address below.

Delete your account inside the app

Open OFF UR ROUTE, go to Account & Security, choose Delete Account, read the confirmation information and confirm the deletion request.

Request deletion without the app

Email offurroute@gmail.com using the email address associated with your OFF UR ROUTE account.

Use the subject OFF UR ROUTE Account Deletion and state that you want your account deleted.

What deletion covers

The deletion process is intended to remove the account and associated cloud account data that OFF UR ROUTE is able and required to delete. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute handling or other legitimate operational requirements.

Local device data

Some preferences or downloaded data may remain locally on a device until the app data is removed by the user or the application is removed. Account deletion does not require OFF UR ROUTE to remotely erase unrelated device storage.

Need help?

Contact offurroute@gmail.com.
